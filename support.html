Frequently asked questions

What file formats does Bookaholic support?

EPUB, PDF, TXT, and Markdown files.

How do I import a book?

Open Bookaholic and use the import option in your library to add a file from your device (for example, via the Files app or the share sheet from another app). The book is added to your on-device library.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device. Your books, reading progress, highlights, vocabulary notebook, and review history are never uploaded anywhere — Bookaholic has no account system and no cloud sync. See the privacy policy for details.

Does the app ever use the network?

Bookaholic works fully offline — it never sends your words, readings, or any reading data anywhere. The only network activity is optional and user-initiated: the first time you translate into a new language, iOS may download an Apple Translation language pack (the translation itself happens on your device). You can manage translation in Settings → Languages & Translation.

Does Bookaholic support Japanese furigana?

Yes. Bookaholic can display furigana readings above kanji and includes built-in dictionary lookup for Japanese, English, and Chinese words.
