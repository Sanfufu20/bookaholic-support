How do I cancel renewal or request a refund?

Bookaholic Plus is an auto-renewing monthly subscription charged to your Apple Account. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Use Apple subscription management to cancel renewal. For a refund, use Apple’s refund portal; eligibility depends on Apple and applicable law. Cancelling a task or deleting your account does not cancel renewal or request a refund.

Frequently asked questions

Which devices, languages and system versions are supported?

Bookaholic runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. The app is available in English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese, and it reads books in Chinese, English and Japanese. On-device narration needs an iPhone or iPad with 8 GB of memory; on other devices you can still read, look up words and review.

What file formats does Bookaholic support?

EPUB, PDF, TXT, and Markdown files.

How do I import a book?

Open Bookaholic and use the import option in your library to add a file from your device (for example, via the Files app or the share sheet from another app). The book is added to your on-device library.

How does on-device narration work?

Storyteller turns a whole book or a single chapter into speech on your device. The free plan includes 30 hours of new audio each month (unlimited with Plus), and finished chapters play offline. The first time, voice files are downloaded, from a few hundred MB up to about 2 GB depending on the language; use Wi-Fi if you can. On iOS 26 and later, generation can continue in the background after you leave the app; iOS may end it early, and finished audio is kept. Full Cast (Bookaholic Plus) gives each character a different voice: it sends the text of the chapters you approve to the cloud to find the characters, and the audio is still generated on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Ordinary reading and standard imports use your on-device library. If you enable iCloud sync, library data is also stored in your private iCloud space. On-device narration generates audio on your device and sends no book text anywhere. Full Cast (Bookaholic Plus) sends the text of the chapters you approve to cloud services for character analysis. Features that some versions may enable, such as AI import or cloud narration, send the content you select to cloud services. Available features and their status are shown in the app. See the privacy policy for details.

Does the app ever use the network?

Local reading, dictionary lookup, notes, vocabulary review and on-device narration (once its voice files are downloaded) work offline. Optional dictionary and Apple Translation language downloads, voice downloads, sign-in, subscription verification, Full Cast analysis (and any other cloud task a version enables) and iCloud sync use the network. Voice files for on-device narration are downloaded through Bookaholic's Cloudflare Worker (or, if it is unreachable, Bookaholic's Cloudflare storage); these requests carry no account, book, or reading data. Cloud tasks you submit may continue after you close the app. Use standard import if you do not want a document processed by an AI service.

How do I restore a subscription or delete my account?

Open Settings → Subscription & Plans for Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription, and Settings → Account & Usage for Delete Account. Deleting your account does not erase your local or iCloud library and does not cancel subscription renewal: Apple keeps billing until you cancel in Apple’s subscription settings. Removing the app alone does not close the account.

What if a cloud task or sync cannot finish?

Keep the original file and your local library. Check the task or sync status in the app before retrying; do not delete your only copy to resolve an error. If you contact support, include the app version and the relevant account or task ID, but do not send passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary book text.

Does Bookaholic support Japanese furigana?

Yes. Bookaholic can display furigana readings above kanji and includes built-in dictionary lookup for Japanese, English, and Chinese words.
