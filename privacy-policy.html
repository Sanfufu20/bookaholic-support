Bookaholic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01 · 繁體中文

Bookaholic is a reading and language-learning app. This policy covers local reading and, in versions where they are enabled, optional accounts, subscriptions, AI import, audiobook generation, and iCloud sync. An unavailable cloud feature does not process your book content.

Your choices

Ordinary reading, standard file import, dictionary lookup, notes, and vocabulary review work locally without an account. Bookaholic does not sell personal data, display advertising, or use cross-app advertising trackers. Choosing cloud processing or iCloud sync changes where the selected data is processed, as explained below.

Local reading and optional downloads

Your library contains imported EPUB, PDF, TXT and Markdown files, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, vocabulary and its source sentences, review history, collections, and preferences. These remain on your device unless you enable iCloud sync or send selected content for a cloud task.

Dictionary lookup uses offline dictionaries. Optional dictionary downloads contact the download host but do not submit your lookup words. Apple Translation processes the selected text on your device; iOS may download language data first. You can manage translation in Settings → Languages & Translation. Apple services are also governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Device backups are separate from Bookaholic sync. Backups you enable through Apple may contain app data and may keep earlier copies.

Accounts, subscriptions, and service records

The candidate version currently uses testing credits for approved accounts; in-app payments are not enabled. Testing access has an expiry and service usage limits and is not a paid subscription. Subscription data described below applies when those features are enabled and used.

  • Sign in with Apple: we verify Apple’s sign-in response and retain the Apple-provided account identifier, your Bookaholic account ID, and the information needed to maintain a session. We do not request your name or email for app sign-in.
  • Subscriptions: Apple processes payment. We retain transaction identifiers, the purchased product, subscription status and expiry, and allowance entries to verify access, restore purchases, process refunds, and avoid duplicate charges. We do not receive your payment-card details.
  • Trial eligibility: a one-time device-verification token is sent to Apple to check trial eligibility. We do not retain that token or a reusable device identifier; an irreversible event key is retained to prevent duplicate monthly claims.
  • Cloud operations: we retain submitted-task identifiers, selected ranges, character or page counts, status, cancellation and retry events, costs, and technical error information. These records support the requested service, allowance accounting, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. They are not a record of every page you read or every tap in the app.

These service records are associated with your Bookaholic account. Connection information, such as an IP address and app version, is processed by service infrastructure to deliver requests and maintain security.

AI import and generated speech

AI import asks for confirmation before uploading a document. Cloudflare operates our account, task, and private file-storage services. For AI import, the selected document is uploaded; the pages or chapter information needed for processing are then sent to OpenAI. For audiobook generation, the selected spoken text and language are sent to the enabled speech provider. Generated text, audio, and timing information are stored so you can retrieve the result and resume a task.

Full Cast (character voices) asks for confirmation before uploading. When you agree, the text of the selected book’s chapters, split into sentences, is sent through our service to OpenAI to identify the characters and who speaks each line. The audio itself is generated on your device; no audio is uploaded. The text is held only while each analysis request runs and is then deleted; the character and speaker results are kept for 7 days so the app can download them, and a cache without book text is kept for 30 days. Storyteller (single narrator) reading on your device does not send book text anywhere.

AI-generated voices are synthetic. This version does not record your microphone or offer voice cloning. You can choose standard import instead of AI import. Sending a book for a task does not submit your entire library, notes, or vocabulary notebook to an AI provider.

The management website shows account, allowance, task, and cost information rather than book text. However, the processing service necessarily handles uploaded content; authorized operational access may be needed for service maintenance, support, or security.

Provider data practices

  • OpenAI: API data is not used for model training by default unless the API account opts in. Safety logs may retain content for up to 30 days, or longer for legal or security reasons. Stored background responses have additional retention. Deleting a response does not establish zero retention for safety logs. See OpenAI’s API data controls.
  • ElevenLabs: this speech provider is being evaluated for the candidate version. Its training opt-out and zero-retention settings have not yet been verified for the service account. If enabled, text and generated audio may be retained, and model-improvement use depends on account settings. See data-use settings and retention information. Do not assume Enterprise-only zero-retention protection applies.
  • Cloudflare: provides service hosting, secure access, and storage. Its handling of infrastructure and security information is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Providers may process data outside your country. We do not promise a particular storage region or zero retention. Provider selection and verified account settings must match the version offered to you.

Optional iCloud sync

If enabled, books and their files, collections, annotations, vocabulary and reviews, reading and listening positions, per-book preferences, and generation records are stored in your private iCloud database. In versions that support audio archiving, you can separately save chapters or enable automatic audio saving (off by default), using your private iCloud storage. Removing a download verifies the iCloud copy first and only removes audio on that device; books, annotations, listening positions and cloud audio are kept for later download. Turning automatic saving off does not delete existing copies. Results within the service retention period can also be downloaded again from the generation service.

iCloud uses the Apple account signed in on your device, which is separate from your Bookaholic account. Cloudflare and the management website do not receive a copy of your private iCloud library. Turning sync off keeps local content and does not itself delete previous iCloud copies. Switching iCloud libraries preserves the previous local library separately.

Retention and deletion

  • Local books and downloads: remain until you delete them. Signing out of Bookaholic does not erase local books or downloaded audio.
  • Cloud results: downloadable generation results are currently available for 30 days. Uploaded source files and processing copies may remain to support interrupted tasks until the associated book or account is deleted. Cancelling processing is not the same as deleting every uploaded copy.
  • Submitted text: the text you submit for a cloud task is deleted from our task records after the task ends: at the next scheduled cleanup once it completes, or 30 days after it last failed or was cancelled, so it can be retried until then. A task whose paid provider request is awaiting confirmation keeps its text until that is resolved, for at most 90 days after its last activity. A task left unfinished for 90 days is cancelled and its text deleted; if a fault prevents that, we resolve it manually. After deletion a task can no longer be retried; generating it again does not charge twice for parts already generated.
  • Full Cast analysis: book text sent for character analysis is deleted when each request finishes, succeeds, or is cancelled; results are deleted after 7 days, and the text-free cache after 30 days. Deleting the book or your account deletes its analysis tasks, cache, and results.
  • Deleting a book: removes it from the active library and requests removal of the associated generation-service results. Offline deletion requests are sent when the appropriate account reconnects. Already-running requests, scheduled cleanup, provider logs, and backups can delay complete removal.
  • iCloud recovery: synchronized deletions can be restored in the app for seven days. Conflict history and recovery copies may remain longer; the candidate version does not guarantee erasure of all historical iCloud copies on day seven. Apple’s iCloud and backup controls apply separately.
  • Deleting your Bookaholic account: use Settings → Account & Usage → Delete Account and complete Apple verification. This removes service access, the Apple sign-in link, tasks, and stored cloud content. Necessary accounting and abuse-prevention records without book text or audio are retained for reconciliation, refunds, and legal obligations. Local and iCloud libraries are not erased by account deletion.
  • Removing the app: removes its data from that device, but does not close your account, erase existing iCloud or backup copies, or cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscription renewal separately in Apple’s subscription settings.

Our service’s deletion actions do not override a provider’s safety, legal, or backup retention. For access, correction, deletion requests, or help with a failed cleanup, contact us below. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account.

Support and policy changes

If you contact support, your email and any information you choose to include are used to respond. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, API keys, or unnecessary book content. Parents or guardians can also contact us about a child’s information.
