Bookaholic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21 · 繁體中文

Bookaholic is a reading and language-learning app for iPhone and iPad. This policy covers local reading, on-device narration and, where you choose to use them, optional accounts, the Bookaholic Plus subscription, Full Cast character analysis, and iCloud sync. It also covers AI import and cloud narration in any version that enables them. A cloud feature that is unavailable does not process your book content.

Operator: Bookaholic. See cloud service information for allowance, cancellation and refunds.

Your choices

Ordinary reading, standard file import, dictionary lookup, notes, vocabulary review, and on-device narration work locally without an account. Bookaholic does not sell personal data, display advertising, or use cross-app advertising trackers. Choosing cloud processing or iCloud sync changes where the selected data is processed, as explained below.

Local reading and optional downloads

Your library contains imported EPUB, PDF, TXT and Markdown files, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, vocabulary and its source sentences, review history, collections, and preferences. These remain on your device unless you enable iCloud sync or send selected content for a cloud task. Audio generated by on-device narration stays on the device that generated it.

Dictionary lookup uses offline dictionaries. Optional dictionary downloads contact the download host but do not submit your lookup words. Apple Translation processes the selected text on your device; iOS may download language data first. You can manage translation in Settings → Languages & Translation. Apple services are also governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

On-device narration generates speech on your device and sends no book text anywhere. Voice files for on-device narration are downloaded through Bookaholic's Cloudflare Worker (or, if it is unreachable, Bookaholic's Cloudflare storage); these requests carry no account, book, or reading data.

Device backups are separate from Bookaholic sync. Backups you enable through Apple may contain app data and may keep earlier copies.

Accounts, subscriptions, and service records

Reading, lookup, notes, review, and on-device narration do not need an account. A Bookaholic account is used only for cloud features such as Full Cast and the Bookaholic Plus subscription. Signing in is optional: if you subscribe or restore a purchase without signing in, the app creates an anonymous account for this device that holds no name, email or Apple identifier; you can add Sign in with Apple later to use Plus on your other devices. Bookaholic Plus is an auto-renewing monthly subscription bought through your Apple Account. Accounts that the operator has invited to test may instead have time-limited testing access, which has its own expiry and service usage limits and is not a paid subscription. The records described below are processed only when you use the related features.

  • Anonymous device account: a random secret kept in this device’s Keychain identifies the account; we store only a one-way hash of it, your Bookaholic account ID, and the information needed to maintain a session. You can delete it in Settings.
  • Sign in with Apple: we verify Apple’s sign-in response and retain the Apple-provided account identifier, your Bookaholic account ID, and the information needed to maintain a session. We do not request your name or email for app sign-in.
  • Subscriptions: Apple processes payment, renewal, and refund requests. We retain transaction identifiers, the purchased product, subscription status and expiry, and allowance entries to verify access, restore purchases, process refunds, and avoid duplicate charges. When several devices share one subscription through the same App Store account, we keep each device’s public model name (for example, “iPhone 15 Pro”, never the name you gave the device) and when it last used the subscription, so the devices sharing it can be listed and managed. After you delete your Bookaholic account, transaction and subscription status records, which contain no name, Apple identifier, or book content, are kept to prevent duplicate billing and the reuse of refunded purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details.
  • Promotional allowances: Signing in with Apple lets you claim a one-time free Full Cast allowance, and later promotions may offer others. So that each Apple ID can claim each promotion only once, we store a one-way hash of your Apple account identifier and when it was claimed. This record is kept after you delete your Bookaholic account, is used only to prevent repeat claims, cannot be turned back into your Apple account, and contains no name, email address, or book content.
  • Trial eligibility (only if a trial is offered): a one-time device-verification token is sent to Apple to check trial eligibility. We do not retain that token or a reusable device identifier; an irreversible event key is retained to prevent duplicate monthly claims.
  • Cloud operations: we retain submitted-task identifiers, selected ranges, character or page counts, status, cancellation and retry events, costs, and technical error information. These records support the requested service, allowance accounting, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention. They are not a record of every page you read or every tap in the app.

These service records are associated with your Bookaholic account. Connection information, such as an IP address and app version, is processed by service infrastructure to deliver requests and maintain security.

AI import and generated speech

Cloudflare operates our account, task, and private file-storage services. In any version that enables AI import, it asks for confirmation before uploading a document; the selected document is uploaded, and the pages or chapter information needed for processing are then sent to OpenAI. In any version that enables cloud narration, the selected spoken text and language are sent to the enabled speech provider, and the consent screen lists the recipients. Generated text, audio, and timing information from these cloud tasks are stored so you can retrieve the result and resume a task.

Full Cast (character voices) asks for confirmation before uploading. When you agree, the text of the selected book’s chapters, split into sentences, is sent through our service to OpenAI to identify the characters and who speaks each line. The audio itself is generated on your device; no audio is uploaded. Text is retained while waiting in the queue and during analysis, then deleted as each analysis unit completes, fails or is cancelled; scheduled cleanup handles interrupted purges. Multi-batch analysis currently has a 72-hour run limit, after which later analysis is stopped and its text cleared; the character and speaker results are kept for 7 days so the app can download them, and a cache without book text is kept for 30 days. Storyteller (single narrator) reading on your device does not send book text anywhere.

AI-generated voices are synthetic. This version does not record your microphone or offer voice cloning. You can choose standard import instead of AI import. Sending a book for a task does not submit your entire library, notes, or vocabulary notebook to an AI provider.

The management website shows account, allowance, task, and cost information rather than book text. However, the processing service necessarily handles uploaded content; authorized operational access may be needed for service maintenance, support, or security.

Provider data practices

  • OpenAI: API data is not used for model training by default unless the API account opts in. Safety logs may retain content for up to 30 days, or longer for legal or security reasons. Stored background responses have additional retention. Deleting a response does not establish zero retention for safety logs. See OpenAI’s API data controls.
  • ElevenLabs: a speech provider that cloud narration could use if it is enabled. Its training opt-out and zero-retention settings have not yet been verified for the service account. If it is used, text and generated audio may be retained, and model-improvement use depends on account settings. See data-use settings and retention information. Do not assume Enterprise-only zero-retention protection applies.
  • Aivis Cloud API: only versions using Aivis cloud narration send selected Japanese text to it; OpenAI may process other supported languages. The consent screen lists the recipients. Aivis states that input text and generated audio are not used for AI-model training, but keeps logs for troubleshooting and service improvement. Its public explanation currently specifies no fixed automatic deletion period and no zero-retention option. See Aivis’s official data-handling explanation.
  • Cloudflare: provides service hosting, secure access, and storage. Its handling of infrastructure and security information is described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Providers may process data outside your country. We do not promise a particular storage region or zero retention. Provider selection and verified account settings must match the version offered to you.

Optional iCloud sync

If enabled, books and their files, collections, annotations, vocabulary and reviews, reading and listening positions, per-book preferences, and generation records are stored in your private iCloud database. Audio generated by on-device narration is not synced; each device generates its own. In versions that support audio archiving, you can separately save cloud-generated chapters or enable automatic audio saving (off by default), using your private iCloud storage. Removing a download verifies the iCloud copy first and only removes audio on that device; books, annotations, listening positions and cloud audio are kept for later download. Turning automatic saving off does not delete existing copies. Results within the service retention period can also be downloaded again from the generation service.

iCloud uses the Apple account signed in on your device, which is separate from your Bookaholic account. Cloudflare and the management website do not receive a copy of your private iCloud library. Turning sync off keeps local content and does not itself delete previous iCloud copies. Switching iCloud libraries preserves the previous local library separately.

Retention and deletion

  • Local books and downloads: remain until you delete them. Signing out of Bookaholic does not erase local books or downloaded audio.
  • Cloud results: downloadable generation results are currently available for 30 days. The original upload for AI import is removed during scheduled cleanup after source text has been permanently purged from every referencing task. Pending or retryable tasks keep their original upload; processing copies follow the text and result retention rules below. Cancelling processing is not the same as deleting every uploaded copy.
  • Submitted text: the text you submit for a cloud task is deleted from our task records after the task ends: at the next scheduled cleanup once it completes, or 30 days after it last failed or was cancelled, so it can be retried until then. A task whose paid provider request is awaiting confirmation keeps its text until that is resolved, for at most 90 days after its last activity. A task left unfinished for 90 days is cancelled and its text deleted; if a fault prevents that, we resolve it manually. After deletion a task can no longer be retried; generating it again does not charge twice for parts already generated.
  • Full Cast analysis: book text sent for character analysis is retained while queued and during processing, then deleted as each analysis unit completes, fails or is cancelled; results are deleted after 7 days, and the text-free cache after 30 days. Deleting the book or your account deletes its analysis tasks, cache, and results.
  • Deleting a book: removes it from the active library and requests removal of the associated generation-service results. Offline deletion requests are sent when the appropriate account reconnects. Already-running requests, scheduled cleanup, provider logs, and backups can delay complete removal.
  • iCloud recovery: synchronized deletions can be restored in the app for seven days. Conflict history and recovery copies may remain longer; Bookaholic does not guarantee erasure of all historical iCloud copies on day seven. Apple’s iCloud and backup controls apply separately.
  • Deleting your Bookaholic account: use Settings → Account & Usage → Delete Account and complete Apple verification. This removes service access, the Apple sign-in link, tasks, and stored cloud content. Necessary accounting and abuse-prevention records without book text or audio are retained for reconciliation, refunds, and legal obligations. Local and iCloud libraries are not erased by account deletion. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription: Apple keeps billing until you cancel in your Apple subscription settings.
  • Removing the app: removes its data from that device, but does not close your account, erase existing iCloud or backup copies, or cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscription renewal separately in Apple’s subscription settings.

Our service’s deletion actions do not override a provider’s safety, legal, or backup retention. For access, correction, deletion requests, or help with a failed cleanup, contact us below. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account.

After account deletion, retained internal account identifiers, allowance entries, transaction/cost and abuse-prevention records contain no book text or audio. They support refunds, reconciliation and legal obligations and are not treated as fully anonymous. We currently set no fixed automatic deletion period for these records. Contact us below to make a data request; we will review the need for retention and applicable obligations.

Local performance diagnostics

The app keeps a small diagnostics log on the device: timings and statuses of narration generation, playback and background work, plus device conditions such as temperature level, battery level, Low Power Mode and how long the device has been on. It never contains book text, titles or author names, and identifiers in it are shortened. The log stays under about 2 MB, older entries are discarded as it fills, and it is never uploaded automatically.

Development and TestFlight builds may also keep performance numbers and system symbols reported by Apple, to investigate responsiveness, memory and failures, and they let you export the diagnostics log as a file or clear it (Settings → Performance Diagnostics). An exported file leaves your device only if you choose to share it. Diagnostics or support information you choose to send is used to resolve that issue.

Support and policy changes

If you contact support, your email and any information you choose to include are used to respond. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, API keys, or unnecessary book content. Parents or guardians can also contact us about a child’s information.
