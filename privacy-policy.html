Bookaholic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-14 · 繁體中文

Bookaholic ("the app") is a language-learning e-book reader with a dictionary-lookup vocabulary notebook and spaced-repetition review. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Short version

Bookaholic does not collect or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. Everything you create in the app stays on your device. The only times any information leaves your device are the two optional features described below: the single word you choose to look up when the optional online-lookup feature is enabled, and the text you choose to translate with Apple's Translation feature. Web searches open in your own browser (Safari), outside the app.

Data stored on your device only

The following are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Books and documents you import (EPUB, TXT, PDF, Markdown)
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, and highlights
  • Your vocabulary notebook, saved words, and the sentences they came from
  • Review history and spaced-repetition schedule
  • Collections, tags, and all app settings

This data is included only in an encrypted device backup if you choose to make one through Apple's iTunes/Finder or iCloud device backup. Bookaholic itself does not run any cloud sync in this version.

Deleting your data

Because everything lives on your device, deletion is entirely in your hands:

  • One book: use the book's ⋯ menu → Delete. This removes the book's files, reading progress, bookmarks and highlights. Words you saved from it that appear in no other book are removed together with their review history.
  • Individual words, highlights or notes: delete them in place from the Vocabulary or Notes tab.
  • Everything: deleting the app from your device deletes all of its data. There is no copy on any server, so there is nothing further to request or erase — and no account to close.

Optional network features

Bookaholic works fully offline by default. Its built-in dictionaries (JMdict, Japanese WordNet, KANJIDIC2, CC-CEDICT and other open data) are bundled in the app. The features below are optional and only send data off your device as described.

Look up online

This feature is turned OFF by default. If — and only if — you turn it on (Settings → Languages & Translation) and then tap "Look up online" on a word the offline dictionaries don't have, the app sends just that word (at most 64 characters, nothing else from the page you are reading) to a third-party dictionary service to fetch a definition:

  • Japanese: Jisho (jisho.org, about page)
  • English: the Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) or Wiktionary (wiktionary.org, privacy policy)

Like every request made over the internet, these requests necessarily carry standard connection information — your network (IP) address and a technical user-agent string. Bookaholic adds no identifiers of its own: no account, no device identifier, no reading history, and no analytics ride along with the word. We receive and retain nothing; the request goes directly from your device to the service. These services are operated by third parties that may keep standard server logs under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature back off — the app then makes no dictionary network requests at all.

Web search

If the online dictionaries have no answer, the app can offer a plain web search for the word. Tapping it leaves the app and opens your own browser (Safari) at jisho.org, Google, or MDBG depending on the word's language. From that point your browser and the site you visit govern what happens; nothing about the search is reported back to Bookaholic.

Apple Translation

Bookaholic also offers an optional Translate feature, built on Apple's on-device Translation framework, and a Chinese-gloss fill-in for saved words that uses the same framework.

When you translate a passage or a saved word's gloss is filled in, the text involved is passed to Apple's Translation framework so it can be translated on your device. The first time a given language pair is used, iOS may need to download that language's Apple translation data over the network. Apple performs this translation and manages these downloads; it is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not this one. You can turn the machine-translation fill-in off in Settings → Languages & Translation.

Data we collect: none

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking.
  • We do not require or offer an account.
  • We do not have access to your library, vocabulary, or reading activity.

Children's privacy

Bookaholic does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
