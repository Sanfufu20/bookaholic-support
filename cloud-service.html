Features and data processing

Ordinary reading, ordinary import, dictionary lookup, notes, vocabulary review and Storyteller (on-device narration, on supported devices) run on your device. Full Cast sends the chapter text within the scope you approve through Cloudflare to OpenAI to identify characters and dialogue; audio is generated on your iPhone or iPad. Where a version enables cloud narration, it sends selected text and language to the providers named before consent. AI import uploads the selected document only when the feature is available and you agree.

Submit only content you have the right to have processed by the relevant services. Books, translations, illustrations, fonts and voices have their own licenses. Possessing a file does not necessarily grant permission for public or commercial use; the app does not grant rights to the work or voice. You can choose local features without submitting a cloud task.

Recipients, retention and deletion are described in the privacy policy. AI character analysis and speech can contain attribution, pronunciation or timing errors. You can inspect results and use available adjustments.

Subscription, testing access and allowance

Bookaholic Plus is an auto-renewing monthly subscription that includes a monthly allowance for Full Cast character analysis. Reading, lookup, notes, review and on-device narration are free and need no account or allowance, and saved audio plays again without using allowance. Accounts that the operator has invited to test may instead have time-limited testing access, which is separate from a paid subscription; the app shows its expiry, available features and service allowance.

The purchase screen (Settings → Subscription & Plans) shows the product, price, billing period, automatic renewal and cancellation information; Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription are on the same screen. Apple processes payment and charges it to your Apple Account, and the subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. AI allowance represents service usage, not an additional payment-card charge. Running out of allowance does not automatically purchase a top-up or incur an overage fee. The allowance resets by calendar month, separately from your renewal date, and the app shows reset times and reasons for pauses. Additional purchases require confirmation in Apple’s purchase flow. Subscription price changes follow Apple’s notifications and applicable rules.

Interruptions, cancellation and retry

Network, device resources, provider availability or usage limits can pause work. Submitted cloud tasks may continue after you close the app. Cancellation may not retract an external request already in progress. Turning cloud consent off stops new tasks; cancel ongoing work separately.

Confirmed completed content is not charged allowance again when the same task is retried. When the outcome of an earlier request is unknown, the service may stop resubmission and require assistance. After source text is deleted under the retention policy, the old task cannot be retried; review the scope and start again. Keep original files: cloud results are not a permanent backup.

Cancel renewal and request a refund

Cancel renewal in Apple subscription management. Request a refund at Apple’s refund portal. Eligibility and review depend on Apple and applicable law. We do not promise approval of every request or exclude statutory rights.

Cancelling renewal, requesting a refund, cancelling a cloud task, deleting an account and uninstalling the app are separate actions. Account deletion or uninstalling does not cancel Apple renewal: Apple keeps billing until you cancel in your Apple subscription settings. For task, allowance or cleanup problems, contact support with the app version and account/task identifiers; do not send passwords or full book text.
